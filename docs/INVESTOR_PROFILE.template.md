# Investor profile (template)

**Do not fill this in inside the repository.** Copy it to a private location outside the repo, for example `~/.config/stock-foresight/investor-profile.md` (directory mode 700, file mode 600), and write your own answers there. It is personal data. Passing it to `brief-explainer` sends it to a model, the same as the brief itself; see the privacy rule in that agent and `docs/DISCUSSION_purpose_and_weekly_brief.md` (Appendix L).

Keep it short, factual and in your own words. Leave a line blank rather than guess. Do not put share counts, cost, purchase dates, account numbers or amounts here.

```
How often I check: <weekly | other>
Number of holdings: <a few | more>
Why I hold each stock: <income | growth | other>   (one line per stock is fine: SAB: income)

Habits I know I have (the ones I want flagged when a brief touches them):
- <for example: I dislike selling below my cost>
- <for example: I check prices at night after reading bad news>
- <for example: I tend to treat past dividends as a reason not to worry>

How I use my written levels:
- <for example: I write them when calm and I want the brief to say plainly when one is reached>
- <for example: I sometimes talk myself out of one; flag it if a level is reached by a small margin>

What I want the explanation to include:
- <for example: the questions I should take to my broker>

What I do not want said:
- <for example: any prediction about price direction>
```
