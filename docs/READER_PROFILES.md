# Simulated reader profiles

Read by the `reader-sim` agent (`.claude/agents/reader-sim.md`). One place to edit them.

These are **simulated caricatures**, written for testing wording in the weekly brief. They are not real people and not the owner. Results from them are not evidence about real investors (see `docs/DISCUSSION_purpose_and_weekly_brief.md`, Appendices D, E, G, J).

**Comparability warning:** Appendices E, G and J used exactly the five texts below. Changing a profile's text breaks comparison with those runs; add a new id instead (P6, P7, ...) and leave P1 to P5 as they are.

| Id | Profile |
|---|---|
| P1 | Novice, 2 years of investing, anxious, hates realising a loss, often checks prices at night. |
| P2 | Experienced, 10 years investing, systematic, writes rules in advance and usually follows them, but dislikes being told what to do by tools. |
| P3 | Retired, income-focused, owns the stock mainly for its dividends, low risk tolerance, decides slowly. |
| P4 | 28 years old, overconfident, trades often, likes decisive signals, easily bored by "do nothing". |
| P5 | Busy professional, 15 minutes a month on investing, trusts the tool's wording, bought the stock on a friend's tip. |

The earlier test runs wrote "SAB" in place of "the stock" in P3 and P5; the meaning is the same.
