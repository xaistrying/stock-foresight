## Full disclaimer

> This panel shows technical observations generated by a statistical model
> trained on historical price patterns (see `docs/MODEL_CARD.md`).
> Confidence reflects the model's own backtested hit-rate, not a guarantee.
> Sentiment reflects technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Ichimoku), not real
> news or market sentiment. Nothing here is investment advice or a
> recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security.
