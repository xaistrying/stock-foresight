## Why

The v5.3 brief's COMPANY block has no code behind it. Simulated readers of the earlier drafts (same-model caricatures: a hypothesis, not evidence) could not tell a registered insider sale from a routine notice, could not size a sale (5 of 5 could not, 2 of 5 sold everything), and doubted a source whose newest item was old (`docs/DISCUSSION_purpose_and_weekly_brief.md`, Appendices F to K). The stored feed already carries what is needed (event code, buy or sell, quantity and window in the title) and the vendor library carries a share count, so this change builds the block from them. It is the last brief piece before the 4-week check, and it depends on the brief package (N1) and the one feed adapter (N2a), not on the gated dividend totals (N2b).

Two measured facts shape it (read-only, 2026-10-12, 53 stored insider rows for SAB and VPB). First, in 46 of 53 the record's publication date is **after** the window's end date, so a bare "planned, not yet executed" would be false for most stored rows; the wording must follow the window against the brief's date. Second, 12 of 53 titles carry no quantity, so a sizing line cannot be the only path. Appendix J also showed the sized line did **not** beat the fallback (1 of 5 sold each, n = 5); the effect of sizing is untested with real people, so the no-size path stays first-class.

## What Changes

Domain-rule honoring (config rule for proposals):

- **Rule 1** honored: dates are anchored on the last stored bar; "in the window" uses calendar days and prints the exact dates (no trading calendar exists).
- **Rule 2** honored: no raw log value is printed.
- **Rule 3** honored: no threshold on a price move is introduced. The fixed numbers added are non-price (a cache age in days, a minimum count of comparison records, a display length cap).
- **Rule 4** honored: no reliability figure is added; the size is a share count ratio, labelled with its basis, not a probability. Every added range sentence says the range ignores the announcement.
- **Rule 5** honored: each source is named for what it reads (company announcement feed from VCI; VCI share count); every third-party title or name is labelled as third-party text, shown in its original language and sanitised; a tag says whether it came from the event record or from a keyword match on a title; the investor's own text is untouched. No language-model text exists here.
- **Rule 6** honored, no rule text edited (owner decision 2026-10-12): tool text never contains buy, sell or hold. A registered trade reads "<name> filed a notice of a sale of <qty> <TICKER> shares (window <start> to <end>; status: not started | under way | ended)" and "a purchase of". The existing buy/sell/hold guard keeps scanning all tool-originated text and exempts only quoted third-party text produced by the sanitiser (besides N1's investor block). There is no frame exemption.

What the change adds (all in the brief package and one new vendor-facing module; no route, no migration, no frontend, no `docs/` edit):

- **COMPANY block** per held ticker: "Needs a look since the last brief", "For information", "Scheduled", from the event record and the news titles through the N2a adapter. Type tags: insider or major-shareholder trade, share issue or capital change, dividend, shareholder meeting, results, personnel or disclosure officer, and "Other announcement, not classified; the title gives no amount". **Never a "routine" label.** Event-record classes come from the event code; keyword classes apply only to titles that start with `<TICKER>:` and are enabled only after a labelled accuracy check on stored titles (only coverage, 97 of 100, was checked before). Everything else falls to the unclassified line. Press items are never classified.
- **Source-health line**: "announcement source answered <date>; <n> items returned; newest <date>", with fixed wording for an old newest item, a failed latest attempt, "never fetched" versus "nothing found".
- **Insider and major-shareholder trade line**: counterparty name from the title text before " - Đăng kí", the notice of a sale or purchase with its quantity, the window with a status (not started, under way, ended) computed against the brief's date, the original-language title as labelled third-party text, "the window runs <n> weeks; this brief covers one" when it extends past the brief, and "The usual range is built from past prices only. It does not account for this announcement, so the real swing could be larger." The event-code class (individual, institution, related person) is **omitted**: the library documents the three codes only as a group, and the split stays unverified.
- **Share count and size**: a new module that calls the vendor's `ratio_summary()` share count, cross-checks it against `overview()["issue_share"]`, caches the last value with its period and fetch time in a state file beside the holdings file, and fetches only when a trade line needs it. It takes the latest `RATIO_TTM` row by (`year_report`, `quarter`), which is a live count and not "as of the quarter". The size line gives the percentage of shares outstanding with the vendor count, its period and its fetch date, and a benchmark against earlier registrations with a stated quantity, each measured against the share count at its own date. When the vendor's counts disagree, or an issue that changes the company's count by 1% or more is dated after the cached fetch or within 30 days before the run (company-wide, built on N2a's issue-class enum; employee issues under 1% are ignored), it shows "size not shown, because the share count is changing (issue on <date>)" and the read-the-notice checklist. Sizing is on by default (the owner accepts the unchecked `ratio_summary()` terms for personal use); a `--no-sizing` option omits sizing and the vendor call entirely.
- **Small share issues**: issues below 1% of the shares (mostly employee issues) are not listed; one quiet line, counts and classes only, says how many were left out (a filter the reader never sees is a risk for the reader; this is a hypothesis from simulated readers, not evidence).
- **Triggers line**: the counts-only `Re-read triggers this run: <label>: <n>[; ...].` line that N1 prints near the top gets further `TriggerCount` entries (registered sales or purchases by a major shareholder or insider, share issues or capital changes, counted since the last brief) and, when needed, a count of holdings whose announcements were not checked. N1's format, label style and banned-word guard are used unchanged; counts only. Depends on N1's line.
- **Footer**: the re-read sentence naming a registered sale or purchase by a major shareholder or insider, a share issue or capital change, or one of the investor's levels appearing or being reached; other new announcements are listed for information.
- **Tests** from fixtures of synthetic rows shaped like stored ones (no real titles, no real holdings, no network): classifier, parser, window states, sizing, benchmark, sanitiser, the one-exemption guard and its negative cases, the small-issue summary, the triggers line, the source-health wording, the cache.

**Not in this change**: dividend, ex-date and share-issue lines for the investor's position (N2a); totals and the post-issue share count and cost (N2b); the event-code class; any model-written text; any verdict; the "biggest weekly move" line; changing any domain rule.

## Impact

- **New files**: `backend/app/services/brief/` modules for announcements, the trade record and sizing, third-party text, the share cache, COMPANY wording and the COMPANY section; `backend/app/services/share_count/fetch.py` (the only new `vnstock` importer, guard first); tests and fixtures under `backend/tests/`; one read-only check script under `backend/scripts/`.
- **Edited**: N1's verb guard test (exemptions and negative cases), N2a's adapter `backend/app/services/brief/feed_source.py` (a separate `Announcement` type and `read_announcements`; N2a's `Event` stays title-free; it remains the only brief file that references the company feed), N1's footer constant and `compose.py` (one entry), N2a's DIVIDENDS section (its Scheduled line moves into COMPANY), the thin script (a `--no-sizing` flag and the share-count wiring). The company-feed package itself gets no new helper; only the spec text is amended.
- **Vendor and privacy**: two more vendor calls per held ticker, only when a trade line needs sizing and the cache is older than 7 days. N1 already sends the held tickers to the vendor on refresh; vnai's analytics may report call arguments (reviewer's reading of the code, traffic not inspected). `ratio_summary()` is a VCI-only route and whether it sits inside the vendor's free-tier terms is unchecked: an open item, with `--no-sizing` as the omission path. The cache file lists tickers, so it gets the holdings file's permissions.
- **Dependencies and ordering**: needs N1 (package, state directory, issued-brief log, guard, and the counts-only triggers line it is adding) and N2a (adapter, `Event`, count-changing-issue rule and cumulative-factor helper, feed freshness). Independent of N2b.
