## Why

The data side of the pivot is shipped (the calibrated range, the data guards, the hardened debate runtime, the aligned labels and disclaimer; all archived 2026-10-07), but the page still has the layout of the retired predictor: a wrapping grid of ticker chips above the chart, a separate range card and a three-level `DebatePanel` stacked in a side column, a second "Searched tickers" list, and a `Load` button next to the search. The owner has approved a design, **Rail · Stage · Verdict** (Claude Design project "Stock Foresight", README, `tokens.json` and three component specs, copied for this draft to the session scratchpad), that replaces it: a fixed topbar over a vertical ticker Rail, a Stage (symbol header with the data date, range tabs, the chart, a 3 agent x 2 round Debate matrix) and a sticky Verdict panel. The design's own status line says the layout, the range tabs, the Rail sort and tags and the Stage header's data date are "not yet built". This change specifies and sequences building it on top of the shipped data, without changing any model, verdict rule or endpoint contract other than one additive catalog field.

Two facts from the live data shape the work. A read-only measurement (2026-10-07, 208 catalog tickers) shows 205 of 208 are `stale` today (age 22 sessions; only 3 are eligible), so the Rail's "why can't it be analysed" tags and the Verdict panel's Insufficient-data state are the common case, not an edge case. And the Rail tags need per-ticker eligibility that `GET /tickers` does not return; calling `assess_eligibility` 208 times costs about 9 to 19 s, a single batched pass about 0.2 to 0.9 s (design.md Decision 3).

## What Changes

- **Layout shell.** `App.jsx` becomes a fixed Topbar (brand, the one ticker search, theme toggle) over Rail, Stage and Verdict at the design's widths (`rail-w` 232, `verdict-w` 340, `topbar-h` 52), with Rail and Verdict sticky and scrolling on their own. Responsive modes at 1440+, 1144 to 1439 (collapsed Rail, overlay on hover or click), 768 to 1143 (Rail drawer, Verdict strip between chart and Debate) and below 768 (one column, search as a combobox, Debate as three tabs), driven by one `useLayoutMode` hook so a component is rendered in exactly one place.
- **Full-width layout.** No `max-width`, no centred column, equal page padding; the Stage takes every pixel left after the Rail (232) and Verdict (340), so the chart and Debate cards grow; the chart is re-laid out to fill its card and the band label stays inside it (design Decision 15; tested at 1440, 1920 and 2560 px).
- **Tokens and theme.** The design's `tokens.json` (colour, type, spacing, radius, layout) becomes CSS custom properties, light and dark, with an explicit `data-theme` toggle in the topbar that falls back to the OS preference. The existing oklch palette, IBM Plex webfonts and `--color-*` names are replaced; the chart re-reads its colours on theme change.
- **Rail.** `TickerPanel`, `TickerChip` and `TickerSearch` are replaced by `Rail`, `RailRow` and a topbar `TickerSearch`: one row per loaded catalog symbol (symbol, "Loaded Nd ago", Refresh on hover/focus and always on touch, a text tag for why it cannot be analysed), a Symbol / Last loaded sort, selection by `aria-current` plus a surface-300 fill and a 2 px accent outline. No stance chip, no return, no status dot. The "Searched tickers" list, its heading and the `Load` button are removed; searched-in symbols that are not in the catalog join the same Rail for the session.
- **One search.** Live filter by symbol; Enter selects a symbol that is in the Rail, loads then selects any other; a distinct message for each of `rate_limited`, `invalid_symbol`, `no_data` (reusing `describeLoadStatus`).
- **Stage.** A symbol header (ticker, last close, "As of D · N sessions old" / "current" / "Stale", from data available before any debate runs), range tabs 3M (60 sessions) / 1Y (about 250) / All (served window) that disable when the ticker has too few sessions, the existing chart with its OHLCV legend and Reset zoom, and the Debate matrix (three agents by two rounds, cards clamped to five lines with Show more, an "Unavailable — did not vote" card). The range band keeps its shipped one-position shape (design.md Decision 6 records the conflict with the design's two lines running from the last close).
- **Verdict panel.** `RangeDisplay` and `DebatePanel` are recomposed into one sticky panel: Typical 5-session move (unsigned, two decimals) with its range check; Analyse (with the "about 45 seconds" notice) or the verdict badge with Agreement, three stance chips, Key tension (the only warn colour), Synthesis, the data line, the report-saved line, the disclaimer with "About this analysis". Six states: ready, running, result, one agent unavailable, insufficient data (also before any run, from eligibility), failed. The shipped runtime behaviours (server-reported stage, elapsed seconds, per-ticker kept result, error messages, `report_saved`) are kept unchanged.
- **Backend (additive).** `GET /tickers` entries gain `eligibility` (`{eligible, reasons, as_of, age_sessions}`, null for an unloaded ticker), computed in one batched pass by a new `assess_eligibility_many` in `data_eligibility.py`; `assess_eligibility` is reimplemented on top of it so the two cannot diverge. No new endpoint, no model change, no write.
- **Request budget.** Unchanged and now tested against the new tree: page load is `GET /tickers` and nothing else; selecting a ticker adds `GET /history` and `GET /range`; the debate runs only on Analyse.
- **Verification tasks.** A contrast check script over the implemented token pairs (3:1 and 4.5:1), a no-direction-cue scan (no signed return, projected point, status dot, up/down colour outside stance and verdict marks, "Confidence"), a request-count check in a real browser, and a 1440 / 1144 / 768 / 390 px visual pass.
- **BREAKING** (UI and tests only): `TickerPanel`, `TickerChip`, `TickerSearch`, `RangeDisplay` and `DebatePanel` as separate components, their CSS and the "Searched tickers" group are removed; the tests that encode them are rewritten (design.md Decision 11). The `GET /tickers` addition is additive; no client breaks.

- **Docs**: `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md` and `docs/DISCUSSION_*.md` are not edited here. This change folds in `DISCUSSION_calendar_staleness.md` (the calendar-aware "Stale" text and age replace the retired dot; no dot is reintroduced) and defers Watchlist curation (`DISCUSSION_post_pivot_review.md` item 8) again.
- **Domain rules** (`openspec/config.yaml`, `CLAUDE.md`; neither is edited):
  - Rule 1 (horizon is 5 trading sessions): honored unchanged. The band and its label say "5-session"; the range tabs count sessions, not calendar days.
  - Rule 2 (no raw log return in the UI): honored unchanged; only `range_5s_pct` is shown, as an unsigned percentage.
  - Rule 3 (magnitude thresholds are volatility-relative): honored; this change adds no threshold on the size of a move. The "Stale" cut-off (3 sessions) is `assess_eligibility`'s provisional constant and is read from the server's `reasons`, never copied into the UI.
  - Rule 4 (reliability is measured, never asserted): honored unchanged. The only reliability figure is the "Range hit-rate", worded "N of the last M five-session moves", shown in one place (the Verdict panel's range block); Agreement is never called Confidence; the band keeps its nominal-coverage sentence and "not a guaranteed interval". The design's README does not mention that sentence; this change keeps it (design.md Decision 8).
  - Rule 5 (label by provenance): honored; agents are Technical Signal, News Context, Macro; language-model text carries the existing note; "Market Sentiment" appears nowhere.
  - Rule 6 (no investment advice, disclaimer): honored and extended: the disclaimer stays in the Verdict panel in every state and is repeated at the Debate matrix, which the design places outside the panel. Verdict labels are the `debate-synthesiser` table, unchanged.
  - No rule needs sign-off to change. Four places where the approved design conflicts with shipped specs or code are listed as Decisions with a recommendation (band shape, band colour, state colours, decimals and wording); none changes a domain rule.
