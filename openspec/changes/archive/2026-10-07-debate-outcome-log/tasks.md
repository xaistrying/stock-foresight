## Apply notes (2026-10-07)

**6.1/6.2 — sample-size figures** (`backend/scripts/verify_log_sample_size.py`, 202 of the 208 modelling-universe symbols with enough shared history):
- (a) ρ̄ = 0.308, effective breadth 3.2. This reproduces Finding 9 of `DISCUSSION_model_direction.md` (0.310, 3.2 of 203).
- (b) Random 25-ticker daily baskets, last 250 sessions (2025-09-29 to 2026-09-30), seeds 0 to 4, 5,700 rows each. Clustered 95% half-width, mean over seeds:

  | stand-in | blocks of 5 | blocks of 20 | blocks of 60 (5 blocks, thin) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | technical vote hit-rate (49.3%) | ±3.3 pts (eff. n about 870) | ±2.7 pts (about 1,400) | ±3.3 pts |
  | volatility-band hit (71.2%) | ±4.1 pts (about 480) | ±5.1 pts (about 310) | ±2.6 pts |

  The naive Wilson half-width is ±1.2 to 1.3 points, so the naive interval is 2 to 4 times too narrow.
- (c) `1.96 * sqrt(0.25 / 150)` = 0.080.
- **Verdict:** the review's "about ±8 points" is **conservative for a nightly 25-ticker basket**; the author's ±3 to 5 holds (±2.7 to 5.1 at the default 20-session block). Caveats: historical proxies, not the debate; the real log starts as a few hand-picked tickers, not a nightly basket, so the review's figure is the safer one to plan with until a basket exists.
- **Adjustments:** none. `BLOCK_SESSIONS = 20` stays (5-session blocks understate dependence between overlapping windows; 60 leaves too few blocks), and `MIN_BLOCKS = 8` stays. At 20-session blocks that is about 160 sessions of daily logging before a clustered interval prints. The report wording already says every interval is a floor.
- **Environment finding:** on Python 3.14.6 + NumPy 2.2.6 + pandas 2.3.3, `DataFrame.pivot` / `unstack` returned a date index with 14 distinct labels for 2,522 dates (values intact, labels wrong). `verify_log_sample_size.py` builds the wide frame with `pd.factorize` and asserts a unique, increasing index. `evaluate_cross_sectional_momentum.py` still pivots, but only uses values, so its figures are unaffected.
