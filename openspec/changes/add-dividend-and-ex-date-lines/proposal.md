## Why

The weekly brief (N1, `add-weekly-holdings-brief`) prints a range and a week move in dividend-adjusted prices. Stored history is back-adjusted (`docs/DISCUSSION_purpose_and_weekly_brief.md`, "Checks run 2026-10-11"), so around an ex-date the brief and the broker app disagree: for SAB, ex 2026-07-28 (3,000 VND), the board showed -7.65% for the window while the stored closes give -1.30%, and the next 5-session range in VND is too high by the dividend (3.00 in the brief's thousand-VND price units) from the ex-date on. Nothing in N1 can say so, because the only source that knows an ex-date, a record date or a share issue is the company feed, and the feed's isolation test and spec forbid any reader outside the snapshot script.

The same feed holds the per-share dividends since the investor's purchase date and the share issues that change a share count. Readers of the simulated briefs asked for them (12 of 20 asked for or computed a dividend sum themselves), and the independent review showed the obvious coverage rule fails (announcement-to-ex lag up to 85 days). This change adds those lines with a stated completeness rule, and nothing that adds dividends up or changes a count.

## What Changes

- **One adapter, `backend/app/services/brief/feed_source.py`**, is the only brief file that references the company feed. It uses the existing read-only `latest_items` and `last_fetch` and adds no code to the `company_feed` package. It returns typed `Event` values (`event_code`, `exright_date`, `record_date`, `payout_date`, `value_per_share` in VND, `exercise_ratio`, `published_at`) and a feed status (never fetched, never succeeded, fetched with a time). It also owns the text of the snapshot command, so no other brief file contains the string `company_feed`.
- **`backend/tests/test_company_feed_isolation.py`: both failing tests are amended** to allowlist exactly that one file (an exact two-file list in the second test); the no-route, no-startup, no-post-run-hook and no-debate/news-agent tests are untouched. New checks: the adapter imports only `company_feed.store`, and importing the brief loads neither `company_feed.fetch`, `snapshot` nor `vnstock`.
- **Brief lines (added to N1's sections):**
  - A **DIVIDENDS** block: cash dividends with `purchase_date < ex-date <= last bar`, per share, with ex-dates, "source covers from <oldest stored event date>", not added up. A completeness rule (`purchase_date >= oldest stored event published_at + MAX_LAG_DAYS`) decides between the plain list and "may be incomplete for ex-dates before <date>". Count-changing share issues since purchase listed separately; a Scheduled line for ex-dates and issues dated after the last bar, bonus or stock-dividend announcements with no ex-date yet, and rights offers ("read the notice; not calculated").
  - Feed freshness: every feed-derived line states when events were last fetched, and says "never fetched" or "last fetch failed" instead of "none found" when that is the case. The brief prints the snapshot command and never runs it.
  - **Ex-date in the window** and **Share issue in the window** lines under NEXT 5 TRADING DAYS (calendar-day approximation, exact date printed; the range shifted down by the dividend, in thousand VND).
  - A **note under the investor's level** when an ex-date fell between the level's entry date and the last bar. The level is never adjusted.
  - **Dividend-aware THE WEEK JUST ENDED**: when exactly one ex-date fell in the last five stored sessions and stored history is adjusted for it, one combined line gives the board move first with the dividend named in the same sentence, then the move excluding the dividend and whether that move was inside or outside last week's range.
  - **Older undated announcements** (owner decision 4): one quiet line, "<n> older announcement(s) with no ex-date are no longer shown.", when bonus or stock-dividend announcements with no ex-date are past 365 days, so a lapsed announcement does not silently look cancelled.
  - **Adjustment status** (patch of 2026-10-12): for each passed ex-date whose bars are stored, ADJUSTED, NOT_YET_ADJUSTED or UNCLEAR from the stored ex-day open against the previous close, with no vendor call. The week note and the level notes say only what that status allows, so nothing depends on an assumed vendor lag.
  - **Week-1 recompute fallback** (moved here from N1): with no issued entry for a holding, percent only, labelled "recomputed today from prices up to <date>; not an issued range", never written to the issued-brief log, and not compared with the move when an ex-date fell in the week or the feed was never fetched.
- **An issue class derived inside the adapter from the stored title** (BONUS, STOCK_DIVIDEND, RIGHTS_OFFER, PRIVATE_PLACEMENT, EMPLOYEE, OTHER; only the enum leaves the adapter, titles are never shown) **and a pure helper for the count-changing rule** (class BONUS or STOCK_DIVIDEND, non-null ex-date, `exercise_ratio > 0`; the first rule, ISS plus ex-date plus ratio, fired for employee issues, a private placement and a rights offer and missed an undated bonus) returning the issues and their cumulative factor. N3 reuses it; N2b builds count and cost arithmetic on it. N2a displays no factor and no count.
- **Spec deltas**: `company-feed` (three requirements modified: the manual-only snapshot's "no other caller" scenario, the read-helper requirement, and "shows nothing"); `weekly-holdings-brief` (requirements added, and three of N1's requirements modified because N2a makes parts of them untrue: the "package does not reference the feed" clause, the week block's no-recompute rule and its "does not say whether an ex-date fell" sentence, and the ban on a DIVIDENDS block). See the sync note below.
- **Out of scope**: the net line, dividends times a running share count, share count and cost per share after an issue (N2b, gated on the 4-week check); announcement tags, "needs a look", insider sizing, shares outstanding (N3); tax, fees, yield; a trading calendar; running or scheduling the snapshot; any API route or UI.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `weekly-holdings-brief`: **created by N1** (`add-weekly-holdings-brief`, artifacts written, not yet applied). This change's delta ADDs requirements (names chosen not to collide with N1's) and MODIFIES three N1 requirements, so it can only be synced after N1 is. Sync order: N1 first, then this change.

### Modified Capabilities
- `company-feed`: "The snapshot runs only by hand" (the "No other caller" scenario: the brief's one adapter is allowed, as a reader of the read helpers, never of the snapshot), "Stored items can be read back with their provenance" (who may read), and "The company feed makes no model call and shows nothing" (the brief may display event dates and numbers, no stored text).

## Impact

- **Backend, new** (under N1's package `backend/app/services/brief/`): `feed_source.py` (adapter, `Event`, feed status, snapshot-command text), `corporate_actions.py` (pure: dividend list, completeness, count-changing issues and factor, pending issues and rights offers, window and scheduled events, shifted range, level-note events), `adjustment_status.py` (pure: ADJUSTED, NOT_YET_ADJUSTED or UNCLEAR), `week_move.py` (pure: week ex-events, calculated board move, week-1 recompute), `dividend_lines.py` (renderers); the fixed sentences are constants in N1's `wording.py`. Tests: `test_brief_feed_source.py`, `test_brief_corporate_actions.py`, `test_brief_week_move.py`, `test_brief_dividend_lines.py`, plus a shared fixture module of event rows.
- **Backend, edited**: `backend/tests/test_company_feed_isolation.py` (two tests amended, new checks added); N1's `MarketFacts` (gains the feed snapshot, stored opens and ex-date statuses), `sections_market.py`, `sections_holding.py`, `compose.py` and `wording.py` (additions only), N1's test that the brief package never mentions the feed, and N1's leakage and verb-guard tests (extended). Design Decision 1 lists the seams. `company_feed/`, `volatility.py`, `range.py` and the debate code are imported from or untouched.
- **Docs at apply time**: `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md` ("Nothing reads it yet except the two helpers" gains the brief's adapter), the status table in `docs/DISCUSSION_purpose_and_weekly_brief.md`.
- **API, frontend, dependencies, schema**: none. The feed log is opened read-only; no write path is added.
- **Domain rules**: none of Rules 1 to 6 is changed. Touched and handled: Rule 1 (an approximation in calendar days only selects which feed dates are printed; the range horizon is still 5 sessions, and the week just ended uses the exact stored sessions), Rule 5 (every feed line is labelled by its source; a recomputed range is labelled as recomputed), Rule 6 (the level note is a separate line so the level line keeps its words limited to reached or not reached and the distance). See design Decisions 9 and 11 and owner decision 1 in the design.
- **Backlog** (`docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`, `docs/DISCUSSION_*.md`): folded in: the feed's irreplaceable-log note (the adapter opens it `mode=ro`), the "none found read as evidence" gap from the independent review (freshness lines). Deferred, not touched: `DISCUSSION_calendar_staleness.md` (no trading calendar is added here either), the archived debate designs' open questions (none touches dividends).
- **Data and licence**: the feed log is vendor data under a personal-use licence and is not to be shared; the test fixtures therefore carry dates and numbers from stored rows and, for issue rows, only the keyword phrase that defines the class as the title.
